# TrafficIQ presentation plan (10–12 slides)

1. Title and project identity.
2. Website traffic problem and motivation.
3. Objectives and strict real-data policy.
4. System architecture and technology stack.
5. Data ingestion and database model.
6. Historical analytics.
7. Calculus: first derivative, second derivative, critical points and integration.
8. Forecasting models and chronological validation.
9. Anomaly detection and capacity planning.
10. Application demonstration and verified test results.
11. Limitations, privacy and future work.
12. Conclusion and questions.

Speaker notes, final screenshots and experimental result slides must be completed after the final application is tested with a genuine dataset.
